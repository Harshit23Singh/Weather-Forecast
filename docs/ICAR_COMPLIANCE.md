# Gram Mausam AI - ICAR & IMD Regulatory & Scientific Compliance

Gram Mausam AI complies with the agricultural meteorological guidelines issued by:
1. **ICAR-CRIDA** (Central Research Institute for Dryland Agriculture, Hyderabad)
2. **India Meteorological Department (IMD)** - Agromet Advisory Services Division
3. **Gramin Krishi Mausam Sewa (GKMS)** scheme.

---

## 1. Threshold Matrix Calibration

| Parameter | Normal Range | Advisory Action Trigger | ICAR Directive Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Max Temperature** | 20°C – 32°C | \(\ge 35^\circ\text{C}\) | Terminal heat stress mitigation via evening sprinkler irrigation. |
| **Relative Humidity** | 45% – 70% | \(\ge 80\%\) & Temp \(\ge 25^\circ\text{C}\) | Fungal vector warning (*Yellow Rust*, *Blast*, *Blight*). |
| **Wind Speed** | 4 – 12 km/h | \(\ge 18\text{ km/h}\) | Immediate suspension of chemical/biological spraying. |
| **Precipitation** | 0 – 15 mm | \(\ge 25\text{ mm (24h)}\) | Clearing field furrow drainage to prevent root hypoxia. |
| **Soil Moisture** | 25% – 40% vol | \(< 18\%\) | Soil water depletion exceeds RAW. Urgent irrigation scheduling. |

---

## 2. Multilingual Agromet Advisory Standards

All automated dispatches adhere to standard bilingual/multilingual formatting structured as:
1. **Weather Summary**: Observed parameters for the specific Gram Panchayat.
2. **Crop Impact**: Growth stage vulnerabilities (Crown Root, Tillering, Flowering, Pod Formation).
3. **Action Directives**: Explicit cultural, irrigation, or chemical management steps.
